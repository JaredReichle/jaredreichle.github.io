About Me

I am a software engineer focused on building fast, reliable systems that automate complex workflows and bridge software with real hardware. My career has centered on developing automation frameworks, integrating distributed services, and delivering production tools that materially speed up development and testing. I've built high-throughput data pipelines, API integrations, and hardware control systems that handle millions of signals per day and directly raise the reliability and velocity of engineering teams.

I am currently completing an M.S. in Computer Science with an AI specialization, where I'm learning to apply machine learning techniques to surface insights from test data, spot patterns that are difficult to detect manually, and strengthen how test cases are designed and validated. The goal is to bring smarter, data-driven decision making into complex system testing.

I'm driven by difficult technical problems and have a consistent history of shipping production-grade solutions that operate cleanly across software and hardware boundaries. My electrical engineering background gives me a practical advantage when designing systems that must interact with real devices or maintain stability under load.

I started as an electrical engineer, but I kept finding myself writing code to solve problems. What began as simple scripts to automate repetitive tasks turned into full systems. I realized I was more excited about building software than designing circuits, so I made the switch to software engineering.

My electrical engineering background wasn't wasted though. It gave me a deep understanding of how hardware actually works, which is invaluable when you're building systems that need to talk to real devices. I can read datasheets, understand signal integrity issues, and debug problems that span both software and hardware layers.

Now I'm working on my Master's in Computer Science with an AI focus, learning how to apply machine learning to make testing smarter. The goal is to use data to find patterns humans miss and build systems that get better over time. It's the perfect blend of my hardware background and my passion for automation.

When I'm not coding, you'll usually find me under the stars or under the hood. Both hobbies scratch the same itch: taking something complex, understanding how it works, and making it do what I want it to do.
